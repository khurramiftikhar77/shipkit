What is a Now / Next / Later roadmap?

A Now / Next / Later roadmap groups work by time horizon instead of exact dates. It was popularised by Janna Bastow of ProdPad as a way to show direction without promising delivery dates that are likely to change.

  • Now: what the team is working on or starting this cycle. High confidence and well defined.
  • Next: what comes after, once Now is done. Reasonably clear, still open to change.
  • Later: bets and problems worth solving that are not planned yet. Low detail on purpose.

Why use themes

Coloring items by theme, such as growth, retention or platform, shows stakeholders at a glance where the team is investing. If one color dominates Now, that is a strategy conversation worth having.

How to use this roadmap builder

  1. Rename the roadmap by clicking its title.
  2. Add items to each column. Write outcomes ("Cut onboarding time in half") rather than features where you can.
  3. Pick a theme for each item and reorder with the arrows.
  4. Click Download PNG and drop the image into your slides, Confluence page or email.

Frequently asked questions

Why no dates on the roadmap?

Dates turn a roadmap into a promise. Time horizons communicate priority and direction while leaving room to learn and adjust.

How far ahead should Later go?

Most teams treat Now as the current quarter, Next as the following one, and Later as anything beyond that.

Can I edit the roadmap later?

Yes. It is saved in your browser automatically, so it is still there when you come back on the same device.

Is the PNG export free?

Yes. The image is drawn in your browser and downloaded directly. There is no watermark beyond a small credit line.
