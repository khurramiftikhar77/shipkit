About ShipKit

ShipKit is a small set of free tools for people who decide what gets built and when: product managers, project managers, team leads and founders.

Why it exists

Most prioritization and planning tools sit behind a signup, a trial or a paid plan, even when all you need is a quick answer for a meeting in ten minutes. ShipKit does one job per page, runs entirely in your browser and lets you copy the result straight into your doc.

Who makes it

ShipKit is built by Khurram Iftikhar, a software engineer working across product, project management and AI. It is a sister project of Meme Maker and DadOps.

How it is funded

The tools are free and funded by a few ads. Your inputs never leave your device, so there is no data to sell.
