Ranking

    What is the RICE framework?

    RICE is a prioritization method created by the product team at Intercom. It scores each idea on four factors so you can compare very different ideas on one scale and explain your choices to stakeholders.

    • Reach: how many people the idea affects in a set period, for example users per quarter.
    • Impact: how much it moves the goal for each person. Use 3 for massive, 2 for high, 1 for medium, 0.5 for low and 0.25 for minimal.
    • Confidence: how sure you are about your estimates. Use 100% for high, 80% for medium and 50% for low.
    • Effort: the total work needed, in person-months, across design, engineering and everyone else.

    The RICE formula

    RICE score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort

    Example: a feature that reaches 2,000 users a quarter, with high impact (2), medium confidence (80%) and 2 person-months of effort scores (2000 × 2 × 0.8) ÷ 2 = 1,600.

    What is ICE scoring?

    ICE is a faster alternative popularised for growth experiments. You rate Impact, Confidence and Ease from 1 to 10 and multiply them. Use ICE when you have many small ideas and no reliable reach data. Use RICE when ideas differ a lot in audience size or effort.

    ICE score = Impact × Confidence × Ease

    How to use this calculator

    1. Pick RICE or ICE at the top.
    2. Add each idea as a row and fill in the scores. The rank and score bar update as you type.
    3. Use the ranking as a starting point for discussion, not a final answer. Strategy, dependencies and deadlines still matter.
    4. Export to CSV for spreadsheets, or copy as a Markdown table to paste into Notion, Jira, Confluence or GitHub.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a good RICE score?

    There is no universal good score. RICE scores only make sense compared with each other inside the same list, using the same reach period and the same effort unit.

    Should reach be per month or per quarter?

    Either works as long as every idea uses the same period. Many teams use a quarter because it matches planning cycles.

    What is the difference between RICE and ICE?

    RICE adds Reach and uses Effort instead of Ease, which makes it better for comparing features with very different audiences. ICE is quicker and works well for growth experiments.

    Is my data saved?

    Yes, in your own browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your browser data removes the list.

    Can I share the results?

    Export a CSV or copy the table as Markdown and paste it into your doc, ticket or chat.
