How much does a meeting cost?

Every meeting has a price: the combined hourly cost of everyone in the room for as long as it runs. A one-hour meeting with six people at 60 per hour costs 360, and if it repeats weekly that is about 18,720 a year. Seeing the number makes it easier to cut attendees, shorten the slot or replace the meeting with a written update.

The formula

Meeting cost = Attendees × Hourly rate × Duration in hours

If you enter a salary, ShipKit converts it to an hourly rate:

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ 1,800 hours

1,800 hours is a common estimate of working hours in a year after holidays and leave. For a fuller picture of company cost, add around 20% to 40% to salaries for taxes, benefits and office costs.

Ways to make meetings cheaper

  • Invite only people who need to decide or contribute. Share notes with everyone else.
  • Default to 25 or 50 minutes instead of 30 or 60.
  • Send an agenda and pre-read so the meeting is for decisions, not reading.
  • Review recurring meetings every quarter and cancel the ones that no longer earn their cost.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use salary or hourly rate?

Use hourly rate for contractors or when you know the blended team rate. Use salary for employees, and ShipKit converts it for you.

Does this include overhead?

No. It uses the rate you enter. To include taxes, benefits and office costs, increase the rate by 20% to 40%.

Is my salary data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and saved only on your device.

Can I use the timer in a real meeting?

Yes. Set the attendees and rate, press Start meeting, and share your screen. It keeps counting while the tab is open.
